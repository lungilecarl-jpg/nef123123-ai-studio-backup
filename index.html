NEF123123 stands for Navigant Economic Freedom. We believe our people must captain our own journey to economic freedom through reporting, teaching and building. Our work centres ownership, opportunity and the power to shape our collective future.

OUR FRAMEWORK

The seven pillars of Navigant Economic Freedom

A FRAMEWORK FOR DISCUSSION & ACTION
01

Economic Freedom

Decoupling from extraction and usurious debt. Advancing sovereign economic choices, fair trade and financial independence.

02

African Wealth Sovereignty

Mineral beneficiation and continental resource retention. Building value chains that keep more value, skills and opportunity on the continent.

03

Land & Food Sovereignty

Supporting equitable access to land, productive agriculture, food security and community prosperity.

04

Industrialisation & Production

Growing African manufacturing, infrastructure, technology and productive capacity.

05

People-Centred Development

Investing in education, skills, health, decent work and the capabilities of our people.

06

Pan-African Unity & Trade

Strengthening continental cooperation, regional trade and shared economic institutions.

07

Democratic Accountability

Promoting transparent institutions, public participation and accountability in the use of collective resources.

Pillars 03–07 are proposed editorial framework wording and can be revised to match NEF123123's formally adopted positions.
